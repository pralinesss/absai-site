Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Abs AI is built so your body photos stay on your phone. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Abs AI is made by an independent developer based in Ukraine (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: absai.support@gmail.com.

Your body photos

  • Stored on your phone. Scan photos and AI previews are saved in the app’s own storage on your device. We don’t upload them to an account or a cloud backup.
  • Sent only to make your results. When you scan, the photo is shrunk and its metadata (such as location and device details) is removed. It is then sent over an encrypted connection to our server for two things:
    • Analysis: an AI model estimates your abs scores and body-fat range.
    • Your AI preview: an AI image model edits a copy of your photo to show a possible “Week 8” version of you. It is shown on the subscription screen.
  • Not kept on our servers. Our server holds the photo only in memory while it handles the request. It doesn’t save it to storage or write it to logs.
  • AI provider. To do this, the photo is sent to OpenAI, our AI provider, under its API terms. Scan requests ask the provider not to store them. The image API used for the AI preview may keep requests for up to 30 days to detect abuse, and then deletes them. Under its API terms, OpenAI doesn’t use this data to train its models.
  • The AI preview is an illustration, not a prediction or a promise. The app labels it “AI visualisation of a possible goal, not a guaranteed result.”

Scores and body-fat estimates

Scores, rankings and body-fat ranges are estimates made by an AI model from one photo. They are not a medical assessment. They are stored on your phone with your scan history.

Your answers and plan

Your onboarding answers (such as age, sex, height, weight, goal, equipment and physical limitations), your plan, workouts and streak are stored on your phone. They are used to build and adjust your plan. They leave your phone only if you turn on the optional backup below.

Optional account and backup

  • You can use Abs AI without an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple (Profile → Account), we keep a backup so you can restore your progress on a new phone.
  • What’s in the backup: your onboarding answers, scan scores and body-fat estimates, your plan, workouts, weight and waist entries, and app settings. Never photos.
  • What Apple shares with us: a user ID and an email address, which can be Apple’s private relay address if you chose “Hide My Email”.
  • Where: in our database hosted by Supabase in the European Union, readable only by your account.
  • Deleting: Profile → Delete account deletes the account and the backup from our servers right away. Signing out stops backups and keeps your data on the phone.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records from Apple.

Anonymous usage data

  • To improve the app, Abs AI counts which steps are reached and whether a purchase starts or finishes (for example “paywall shown” or “scan completed”), and records when the app shows an error screen.
  • These events carry a random ID made on your phone, not your name, email, photos, scores or answers. We don’t use them for advertising, don’t share them with data brokers, and don’t look up your location from your IP address.
  • They’re sent to our analytics provider, PostHog, on its EU servers.
  • Turn this off at any time: Profile → Share anonymous usage data.

Abs AI doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites and doesn’t show ads.

Why we use your data

  • To provide the app you asked for (scans, plan, workouts, backup, subscriptions).
  • With your consent for the scan photo and, where required, anonymous usage data. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app.
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it, using anonymous data only.

Service providers and transfers

We use OpenAI (AI analysis and previews), Supabase (servers, optional backup), RevenueCat (subscription status), PostHog (anonymous usage data) and Apple (sign-in and payments). Some of them process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires it, these transfers are covered by safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Photos: on your phone until you delete them or the app. Not stored on our servers.
  • Backup: until you delete your account.
  • Anonymous usage data: kept by PostHog in line with its retention settings, at most as long as needed to understand how the app is used.

Your choices and rights

  • Profile → Delete all photos removes every scan photo and AI preview and keeps your scores and plan.
  • Profile → Delete all data removes scans, photos, previews, your plan and settings from the app at once.
  • Profile → Export my data gives you a copy of your data as a file (without photos).
  • Profile → Delete account removes your backup from our servers.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or Ukraine), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us to use these rights.

Age limit

Abs AI is for adults aged 18 and over. Don’t upload photos of anyone else, or of anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

Contact
