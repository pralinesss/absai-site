Support

Need help? Email us at absai.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days.

My subscription isn’t showing

Open the paywall or Profile and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Abs AI → Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Tips for a good scan

  • Bright, even light from the front. No strong light behind you.
  • Phone at chest height, about 1.5 m away, shoulders level.
  • Same pose, light and time of day for every rescan, so scores are comparable.

Where are my photos?

Only on your phone. They’re sent to our server only to analyse your scan and make your AI preview, and aren’t kept there. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Delete all photos, Delete all data, or Delete account (if you signed in with Apple). Deleting the app also removes everything stored on your phone.

Moving to a new phone

Sign in with Apple in Profile → Account before you switch, then sign in on the new phone to restore your plan and progress. Photos aren’t backed up, by design.
